Step 1 of 9

Built and numerically verified in Wolfram Mathematica (34 automated checks: matrix dimensions, softmax sums, causal-mask behaviour, gradient sign, that P(“shoe”) rises while loss falls after the update, and that the same holds when retargeted at “cement” — see the full notebook). This page reimplements those exact, already-checked equations in plain JavaScript — nothing here is estimated or re-derived independently.

What this toy model leaves out on purpose, and why: LayerNorm, residual connections, dropout, multi-head attention, and a large feed-forward network — all omitted because, at this scale (1 head, 1 block, tens of parameters), they would add notation without adding insight (the "Show the maths" panel above includes a small optional illustration of what the feed-forward layer's nonlinearity would add). Positional encodings get a sharper caveat: self-attention has no built-in sense of order, and the causal mask above only distinguishes position 1 from position 2 because, with exactly 2 tokens, there are only 2 possible masks — it says what a position may look at, not where it sits. Longer sequences need an explicit positional signal (learned embeddings, sinusoidal encodings, or RoPE). See methodology/ in the project repository for the full reasoning, or the full notebook, statically rendered, for every derivation this page's numbers come from.
