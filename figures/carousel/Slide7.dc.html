Positional encodings: self-attention has no built-in sense of order — a causal mask only tells position 1 from position 2 because, with 2 tokens, there are only 2 possible masks. It says what a position may look at, not where it sits. Longer sequences need real position signals: learned embeddings, sinusoidal encodings, or RoPE.
